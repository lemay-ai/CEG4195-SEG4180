# Rideau Makerspace: Member Handbook

The Rideau Makerspace is a fictional community workshop, invented for the course's RAG
example (week4-rag-pipeline.ipynb). None of these rules, prices or error codes are real,
so a language model cannot know them unless it retrieves them from this file.

## Opening hours

The makerspace is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays and from 10 am to 6 pm on
weekends. It is closed on statutory holidays. Members with the Night Owl add-on may stay
until 1 am on weekdays, but never alone: at least two members must be in the building
after 10 pm.

The front desk is staffed from 9 am to 5 pm on weekdays. Outside those hours, members
open the door with their key fob.

## Memberships and fees

A standard membership costs 65 dollars a month. Students with a valid student card pay
35 dollars a month. A day pass costs 20 dollars and includes the hand tools and the
electronics bench, but not the laser cutter, the CNC router or the wood shop.

The Night Owl add-on costs 15 dollars a month. Memberships renew on the first day of each
month. To cancel, email the front desk before the 25th of the month, or the next month is
charged.

## Guests

Each member may bring up to two guests at a time. Guests sign the waiver at the front desk
before they touch any tool. Guests may use the hand tools and the electronics bench under
the member's supervision. Guests may not use the wood shop, the laser cutter or the CNC
router, even with a member present.

## Safety training

Every new member takes the general safety orientation, a 90-minute session held every
Tuesday at 7 pm. Each large machine also needs its own sign-off: the laser cutter sign-off
takes one hour, the wood shop sign-off takes three hours, and the CNC router sign-off takes
two sessions of three hours. Sign-offs are booked on the members' portal.

Safety glasses are required in the wood shop and at the CNC router at all times. Hearing
protection is required when the planer or the dust collector is running. Long hair is tied
back and no loose sleeves or jewellery are worn near rotating tools.

## Laser cutter

The laser cutter is booked in 30-minute slots, at most two slots per member per day.
Allowed materials: plywood up to 6 mm, cardboard, paper, felt and cast acrylic. Never cut
PVC or vinyl: they release chlorine gas that harms people and corrodes the machine. Never
cut polycarbonate or fibreglass. Never leave the laser cutter while it is running; a fire
can start in seconds. The red fire blanket hangs on the wall to the left of the machine.

The laser cutter costs 1 dollar for each minute of cutting time, charged to the member's
account.

## 3D printers

The makerspace has six 3D printers. Printing is free for the first 200 grams of filament
each month; after that, filament costs 5 cents a gram. Prints longer than 8 hours must
start before 2 pm so that staff can check them before closing.

Printer error codes:

- E12: the nozzle did not reach its temperature. Check that the heater cable is plugged in,
  then restart the print.
- E37: the filament ran out or jammed. Load a new spool, then press Resume; do not cancel
  the print.
- E51: the bed is not level. Run the automatic levelling from the Maintenance menu.
- E88: the printer lost its connection to the print server. Copy the file to a USB key and
  print from the key.

## Wood shop

The wood shop has a table saw with a flesh-sensing brake, a bandsaw, a planer, a drill
press and two lathes. If the table saw brake fires, the blade and the brake cartridge must
be replaced by staff: report it at the front desk and do not try to reset it. The
replacement is free the first time; after that, the member pays 120 dollars for the
cartridge.

Clean up your sawdust before your booking ends. The shop vacuum is in the closet next to
the bandsaw.

## Storage and lockers

Small lockers cost 10 dollars a month and large lockers cost 25 dollars a month. Projects
left on a shared workbench overnight are moved to the "orphan shelf" and recycled after
14 days.

## Lost key fobs

A lost key fob is replaced at the front desk for 15 dollars. The old fob is switched off
as soon as the loss is reported.
